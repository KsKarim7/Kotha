# Sprint 2026-W47: Milestone 1 review

## Goal

Finish Milestone 1, "browser call works": VAD, ASR, RAG, LLM and TTS end to end in a browser call,
with every reported number coming from a committed evaluation run.

## Planned

The MS1 modules: M1 audio and test set, M2 VAD, M3 ASR and its evaluation, M4 the knowledge base
and hybrid retrieval, M6 answer-only dialogue, M7 TTS, and M8 the WebSocket call loop, browser
client, latency harness and Docker stack.

## Done

- **M1 to M3** (PRs #16 to #40): the audio helpers and telephony simulator; Silero VAD and the turn
  detector; the faster-whisper wrapper with its decoding settings (ADR 0005); the WER tooling
  (normaliser, scoring, loanword-agnostic score, error analysis, MLflow); the test-set guideline,
  manifest validator and reference lint (ADR 0002).
- **M4** (#41 to #50, #53): sixteen bilingual knowledge-base documents, the chunker, PostgreSQL with
  pgvector, bge-m3 ingestion, hybrid retrieval with RRF (ADR 0006), the retrieval evaluation and its
  first results, and the citation and number verifier.
- **M6 answer-only** (#51, #52): the Gemini client (ADR 0007) and the answer pipeline with its
  guardrails (ADR 0008), a text chat and a scenario runner.
- **M7** (#54 to #56): the MMS voices (ADR 0009), speakable text, and the TTS service with a blind
  listening test ready to rate.
- **M8** (#57 to #62): the call loop on the blueprint's WebSocket protocol (ADR 0010), warm-up and
  readiness, the browser page, the latency harness and its first results, and the Docker stack,
  checked from a fresh clone.

## Slipped

- The recorded `asr_test` set, and with it the ASR WER table, the live ASR model choice (M3-6), the
  VAD hangover ablation and the ASR-transcribed retrieval column.
- The TTS listening scores and a committed RTF run.
- A real microphone call in a browser.

## Why

The recordings need consenting speakers and the listening test needs three listeners, which only
Khaled can arrange. The browser page could not be run here: the work happened without a browser,
so its JavaScript was reviewed by hand but never executed.
