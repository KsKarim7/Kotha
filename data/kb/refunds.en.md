# Refunds

## When we refund a charge

Nodi Telecom refunds a charge only when the charge was wrong. There are three kinds of wrong charge:

- **A pack charged but not activated.** The price of a pack was taken from your main balance, but
  the pack never became active: it does not appear in your data balance or minute balance, and you
  did not receive the SMS that confirms a purchase.
- **A double charge.** The same purchase was charged twice, for example one pack bought once but taken
  from your main balance two times.
- **A charge that a complaint investigation finds was wrong.** Any other charge that customer care
  investigates after your complaint and finds should not have been made, for example a call charged
  from your main balance although it was covered by the minutes in your voice pack.

In each case, the refund is the amount that was charged wrongly, no more and no less. Customer care
tells you the amount when the refund is approved.

Before you ask for a refund for a pack that seems not to have been activated, check your data balance
with `*747*2#` or your minute balance with `*747*3#`: sometimes the pack is active and only the
confirmation SMS is late. The document on buying, activating and stopping packs explains what a
successful purchase looks like.

## When there is no refund

There is no refund for data, minutes or packs that were used as described in the other documents.
In particular, nothing is refunded for:

- a pack whose validity ended with data or minutes left: unused data and minutes expire with the pack;
- a pack whose data or minutes ran out before the end date;
- a pack that you stopped yourself: stopping a pack forfeits whatever is left in it;
- a pack that you bought by mistake and that was activated as you asked;
- a pack renewed by auto-renew while auto-renew was on;
- calls, SMS and data charged correctly from your main balance, including roaming charges at the
  rates shown in the app.

These rules are the same as in the documents on internet packages, on voice packages, on combo
packages and on buying, activating and stopping packs.

Two other situations are handled differently. A recharge to the wrong number is not a refund: report
it within 24 hours as the document on recharge problems describes, and remember that a reversal is
not promised. A card or wallet payment that was taken although the recharge failed is returned by the
payment provider, not by Nodi Telecom; the document on recharge problems explains that as well.

## How to ask for a refund

You ask for a refund by making a complaint. There is no separate refund form. Make the complaint on
the helpline, in the Help section of the Nodi Telecom app or at a service centre, as the document on
complaints describes, and say that you are asking for a refund.

Ask within 30 days of the charge. After 30 days, the details needed to check a charge may no longer be
complete, and a refund may not be possible.

Have these details ready:

- your Nodi Telecom number;
- the date and time of the charge;
- the pack code, or what the charge was for;
- what the history of your main balance in the app shows;
- any SMS you received about the charge.

You receive a ticket number by SMS, in the form `TKT-` followed by 6 digits. Customer care
investigates the charge and replies within 3 working days, as for any complaint. The reply tells you
whether the refund has been approved, and if not, why not. If you do not agree with the answer, you
can escalate the complaint as described in the document on complaints.

## How a refund is paid

An approved refund is always paid back to your main balance. It is never paid in cash, to a bank
card or to a mobile wallet, even if you recharged with a card or a wallet.

The refund reaches your main balance within 7 days of the approval, and usually much sooner. You
receive an SMS when it has been added, and the refund appears in the history of your main balance in
the app. You can check your main balance at any time with `*747*1#`, in the app, or by sending `BAL`
to `7470`.

A refund is added to your main balance like any other credit, and you can use it for packs, calls and
SMS straight away. It is not a recharge, so it does not restore the 90 days of main account
validity; the document on validity explains how that works.

If the refund has not arrived 7 days after the approval, call the helpline and give your ticket
number. Customer care checks the refund and tells you what happens next. Nodi Telecom never asks for
a PIN, a password, a one-time code or a payment in order to pay a refund.
