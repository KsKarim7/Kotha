# Unused data and rollover

## Is there a rollover?

No. Nodi Telecom has no data rollover and no minute rollover. When a pack's validity ends, any data
or minutes left in it expire together with the pack. They are not carried over to the next pack, to
another pack you already have, or to your main balance, and they cannot be refunded.

This is the same for every pack: internet packs, the night pack, voice packs and combo packs. It is
also the same when you stop a pack yourself: stopping a pack ends it at once, and whatever is left in
it expires immediately, without a refund. The documents on internet packages, on voice packages and
on buying, activating and stopping packs describe these rules in full.

Some people use the word rollover for different things, so here is what does not happen on Nodi
Telecom:

- data left at the end of a month is not added to next month's pack;
- data left in one pack is not moved to another pack when the first one ends;
- minutes left in a combo are not kept after the combo ends;
- unused data is not turned into main balance.

There is only one way to keep unused data or minutes, described in the next section. To avoid losing
data, check your data balance a day or two before a pack ends, with `*747*2#`, in the app, or by
sending `DATA` to `7470`. The answer shows the data left and the end date and time of every pack.

## Keeping unused data or minutes

The only way to keep data or minutes that you have not used is to buy the same pack again before it
ends. In that case the two purchases are combined into one pack:

- the data or minutes left from the first purchase are kept, and the new data or minutes are added
  to them;
- the validity becomes the later of the two end dates, which is the end date of the new purchase.

For example, if you have 4 GB left in NT-30GB with 5 days to go and you buy NT-30GB again, you will
have 34 GB, valid for 30 days from the second purchase. For a combo, the data and the minutes are
each added up in the same way, and both parts share the later end date.

This works only with the same pack, and only while it is still active. Buying a different pack, for
example NT-15GB while you still have data in NT-30GB, does not combine anything: the two packs run
side by side, each with its own data and its own end date, and the pack that ends soonest is used
first. Buying a pack again after the first one has ended does not bring back the data that expired.

The second purchase costs the current price of the pack, taken from your main balance. Prices are
shown in the Nodi Telecom app, and customer care can tell you the current price on the helpline.

## Auto-renew does not roll anything over

Auto-renew buys the same pack again automatically when its validity ends. It is off by default, and
you can turn it on or off in the app, with `*747*4#`, or by sending `AUTO ON` or `AUTO OFF` and the
pack code to `7470`, as the document on buying, activating and stopping packs explains.

Auto-renew is not a rollover. It renews the pack at the moment its validity ends, and at that moment
the old pack has already ended and its unused data or minutes have already expired. The renewed pack
starts with its normal data or minutes and a new end date; nothing from the old pack is added to it.

So if you want to keep unused data, auto-renew does not help: buy the same pack again before it ends
instead. Auto-renew is useful for a different reason: it makes sure you are not left without a pack
when the validity ends.

Auto-renew also does not renew a pack early when its data or minutes run out before the end date. In
that case the pack stays empty until its end date, and you can buy the same pack again if you need
more before then; the amounts add up and the later end date applies, as described above.
