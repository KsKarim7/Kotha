# Internet packages

## Internet packs

Nodi Telecom offers six internet packs. Each pack gives a fixed amount of data for a fixed number
of days, counted from the moment the pack is activated. The table lists every pack with its code,
its data and its validity. Use the code when you buy a pack by SMS or when you ask customer care
about it.

| Code | Data | Validity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NT-1GB | 1 GB | 3 days | A small pack for a few days of light use |
| NT-5GB | 5 GB | 7 days | A weekly pack for everyday browsing |
| NT-15GB | 15 GB | 30 days | A monthly pack for regular use |
| NT-30GB | 30 GB | 30 days | A monthly pack for video and heavy use |
| NT-60GB | 60 GB | 30 days | Our largest monthly pack |
| NT-NIGHT-5GB | 5 GB | 7 days | Works only from 00:00 to 08:00 |

All internet packs work on 4G and 3G wherever Nodi Telecom has coverage. The data can be used for
browsing, social media, video, app downloads and calls made over the internet. A pack is valid
only on the number it was bought for, and its data cannot be moved to another number. If more than
one pack is active at the same time, the pack that ends soonest is used first.

## When the data or the validity runs out

When the data in your pack is used up, your internet connection stops. Nodi Telecom does not
charge your main balance for internet use after a pack runs out, so there is never a surprise
charge. You receive an SMS when 80 percent of the pack's data has been used, and another SMS when
the data is finished. To keep browsing, buy a new pack. Auto-renew does not help here: it renews a
pack only when its validity ends, not when its data runs out.

When the validity of a pack ends, any data left in it expires. Unused data does not carry over to
the next pack, and it cannot be refunded. The end date and time of each pack are shown when you
check your data balance, so it is worth checking a day or two before a pack ends.

If you buy the same pack again before it ends, the data left in it is kept and the new data is
added to it, and the validity becomes the later of the two end dates. The details are in the
document on buying, activating and stopping packs. If you buy a different pack, both packs run
side by side, and the one that ends soonest is used first.

## The night pack

The night pack, NT-NIGHT-5GB, gives 5 GB of data that can be used only between 00:00 and 08:00,
Bangladesh time. Outside those hours the night pack is paused: its data stays in the pack but is
not used. If you have another internet pack at the same time, that pack is used during the day and
the night pack is used at night.

If you have no other active internet pack, your internet connection stops at 08:00 and starts again
at 00:00, as long as the night pack still has data and has not reached its end date. The night
pack is valid for 7 days from activation, counted in full days and not in nights. A night pack
bought at 23:00 therefore ends seven days later at 23:00.

The night pack suits large downloads, app updates and videos that can wait until night. When you
check your data balance, the night pack is shown on its own line, separate from the day-time packs,
with the data left and its end date. Buying the night pack twice works like any other pack: the data
is added up and the later end date applies.

## Prices and how to buy

Prices of packs can change, so this page does not list them. The current price of every pack is
shown in the Nodi Telecom app, and customer care can tell you the current price on the helpline.
The price is taken from your main balance when the pack is activated, so check that your main
balance is enough before you buy.

You can buy any internet pack in three ways:

- In the Nodi Telecom app, open the packs page, choose the pack and confirm.
- Dial `*747*4#`, choose internet packs, then choose the pack and confirm.
- Send the pack code, for example `NT-30GB`, by SMS to `7470`.

After a successful purchase you receive a confirmation SMS with the pack's data and its end date.
If the purchase fails, nothing is taken from your main balance, and you receive an SMS that explains
why, for example that your main balance is too low. For stopping a pack or turning auto-renew on and
off, see the document on buying, activating and stopping packs. All codes and numbers are listed
under "How to reach us" in the document on checking your balance.
