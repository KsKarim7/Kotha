# Service hours

## Helpline and voice assistant

The Nodi Telecom helpline and its voice assistant are open 24 hours a day, every day of the year,
including Fridays and public holidays. Call `747` from a Nodi Telecom number, or `01700000009` from
any other phone, including from abroad. Calls to `747` are free.

The voice assistant answers first. It can answer general questions about packs, recharges, validity
and the other topics in these documents at any time. For questions about your own account, such as
your balance or your active packs, it first checks your phone number and your account PIN, as the
document on account PIN and verification describes. If the voice assistant cannot help, or if you ask
for a person, it passes the call to customer care, who are also available around the clock.

At busy times, for example in the evening and on the first days of a month, you may have to wait
before customer care answers. The voice assistant tells you the expected waiting time. For simple
things such as checking your balance, the USSD codes and the app are faster than the helpline.

You can block a lost or stolen SIM through the helpline at any hour, from any other phone, as the
document on a lost or stolen SIM explains. Making a complaint through the helpline is also possible
at any hour; the reply is counted in working days, as the document on complaints describes.

## Service centres

Nodi Telecom service centres are open from Saturday to Thursday, from 10:00 to 18:00. They are closed
on Fridays and on public holidays. A customer who is already waiting inside at 18:00 is still served.

Some services are available only at a service centre, because they need the registered owner in
person with their own national ID card:

- replacing a SIM, as the document on replacing a SIM describes;
- unblocking a SIM that was blocked after it was lost or stolen;
- resetting your account PIN if you cannot use the app.

Service centres can also take complaints and help with any other question about your number. They do
not sell recharges and do not handle payments other than the SIM replacement fee.

Bring your own national ID card, the original and not a copy, for any service that concerns your
account. Queues are usually shortest in the morning, soon after opening, and on weekdays in the
middle of the month. If you are not sure whether a visit is needed, ask customer care on the helpline
first; many questions can be answered on the phone or in the app.

## The app, USSD and SMS

The Nodi Telecom app, the USSD codes and the SMS short code are available at any time, 24 hours a
day, every day, except during announced maintenance. They work from your own number, so you can use
them without calling anyone:

- check your main balance with `*747*1#`, your data balance with `*747*2#` and your minute balance
  with `*747*3#`;
- buy, stop or auto-renew a pack with `*747*4#`;
- recharge with a voucher with `*747*5*<voucher number>#`;
- send `BAL`, `DATA`, `MIN`, a pack code, `STOP`, `AUTO ON` or `AUTO OFF` to `7470`.

The app does all of this as well, and also shows the history of your recharges and charges, lets you
set and change your account PIN, turn roaming on and off, and make a complaint in the Help section.
All codes and numbers are listed under "How to reach us" in the document on checking your balance.

Recharging at retailers depends on each shop's own opening hours, which Nodi Telecom does not set.
Recharging in the app and from a mobile wallet works at any time.

## Public holidays and maintenance

On public holidays the service centres are closed, but the helpline, the voice assistant, the app,
USSD and SMS work as usual. Public holidays do not count as working days, so they are not counted in
the 3 working days within which Nodi Telecom replies to a complaint.

From time to time the app, USSD or SMS services need maintenance. Maintenance is always announced at
least 24 hours in advance, by SMS and in the app, with the date, the start time and the expected end
time. Maintenance is usually done at night, when few customers use the services.

During maintenance, some functions of the app, USSD or SMS may not work for a while. Calls, SMS
between phones and data on your packs keep working as usual, and the helpline stays open, so you can
still call customer care. If something does not work after the announced end time, wait a few
minutes and try again, and if it still does not work, call the helpline.

Unannounced interruptions can happen, for example after a power failure. In that case, Nodi Telecom
sends an SMS when the service is back. A problem with the network in your area is not maintenance: if
calls or data stop working where you are, call the helpline or make a complaint.
