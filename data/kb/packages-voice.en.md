# Voice packages

## Voice packs

Nodi Telecom offers two voice packs. Each pack gives a fixed number of call minutes for a fixed
number of days, counted from the moment the pack is activated.

| Code | Minutes | Validity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NV-100 | 100 | 7 days | A weekly pack for regular calls |
| NV-300 | 300 | 30 days | A monthly pack for frequent callers |

Minutes are counted per second, so a call of one minute and ten seconds uses seventy seconds of
your pack, not two full minutes. You receive an SMS when 80 percent of the pack's minutes have been
used, and another SMS when the minutes are finished. A pack is valid only on the number it was
bought for, and its minutes cannot be moved to another number.

If more than one voice pack is active, the pack that ends soonest is used first. Minutes from a
combo pack are counted in the same way; see the document on combo packages. When all your pack
minutes are finished, calls are charged from your main balance at the standard call rate. The
current call rate is shown in the Nodi Telecom app, and customer care can tell you the rate on the
helpline. Unused minutes expire when the pack's validity ends and do not carry over.

## Which calls the minutes cover

The minutes in a voice pack cover local calls inside Bangladesh to any mobile or landline number.
That includes calls to other Nodi Telecom numbers (on-net) and calls to numbers of every other
operator in Bangladesh (off-net). On-net and off-net calls use the same minutes, at the same rate
of one pack minute for each minute of calling.

The minutes do not cover:

- international calls, to or from numbers outside Bangladesh;
- calls made while roaming abroad;
- SMS and MMS;
- calls to premium-rate and value-added service numbers;
- voice or video calls made over the internet in apps, which use data instead.

Calls that are not covered are charged from your main balance at the standard rate for that kind of
call, even while your voice pack still has minutes. Calls to the Nodi Telecom helpline `747` are
free and do not use pack minutes. If you are not sure whether a call is covered, check your minute
balance before and after a short call, or ask customer care.

## Prices and how to buy

Prices of voice packs can change, so this page does not list them. The current price of each pack
is shown in the Nodi Telecom app, and customer care can tell you the current price on the helpline.
The price is taken from your main balance when the pack is activated.

You can buy a voice pack in three ways:

- In the Nodi Telecom app, open the packs page, choose the voice pack and confirm.
- Dial `*747*4#`, choose voice packs, then choose the pack and confirm.
- Send the pack code, for example `NV-300`, by SMS to `7470`.

After a successful purchase you receive a confirmation SMS with the number of minutes and the end
date. If the purchase fails, nothing is taken from your main balance. You can check the minutes
left at any time with `*747*3#`, in the app, or by sending `MIN` to `7470`. For auto-renew and for
stopping a pack, see the document on buying, activating and stopping packs.
