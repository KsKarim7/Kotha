# A lost or stolen SIM

## Block your SIM at once

If your SIM or your phone is lost or stolen, block the SIM at once. Someone who has your SIM can make
calls, send SMS, use your data and receive messages meant for you, so every minute counts.

Your own SIM is no longer with you, so call the helpline from another phone:

- from another Nodi Telecom number, call `747`;
- from any phone, including a phone of another operator or a phone abroad, call `01700000009`.

The helpline and the voice assistant are open 24 hours, every day, so you can block a SIM at any
time, including at night, on Fridays and on public holidays. Tell customer care the number you want
to block and that the SIM is lost or stolen. Customer care asks a few questions about the number to
make sure the request is genuine, and then blocks the SIM. The block takes effect within a few
minutes, and customer care tells you when it is done.

Blocking a SIM is always possible from the helpline, even if you have forgotten your account PIN.
It only stops the SIM from being used, so it cannot harm the owner. If the phone was stolen, you may
also want to report the theft to the police; Nodi Telecom does not need that report to block or
replace the SIM.

## What blocking does

Once the SIM is blocked, it can no longer be used for anything:

- no calls can be made or received;
- no SMS can be sent or received;
- no data can be used;
- the number cannot be recharged, by any method.

Blocking also means that nothing can be done on the account from the blocked SIM itself, including
calls to the helpline. A blocked SIM is usually in someone else's hands, so it is never used to check
who you are or to change anything.

Your account itself is kept as it was:

- your number stays yours;
- your main balance is kept;
- your active packs are kept, with their data or minutes left.

Blocking does not pause anything. Your packs keep their end dates and expire normally while the SIM
is blocked, and your main account validity keeps counting, as described in the document on validity.
To use your balance and packs again, replace the SIM as soon as you can.

## Getting a new SIM

After blocking, get a new SIM with the same number at a Nodi Telecom service centre. Replacing a SIM
works as described in the document on replacing a SIM:

- only the registered owner of the number can do it, in person;
- bring your own national ID card, the original and not a copy;
- the new SIM keeps your number, your main balance and your active packs;
- a replacement fee may apply, and customer care or the app can tell you the current fee.

The new SIM is usually active within an hour after it is handed to you. From that moment the old SIM
can never be used again, even if it is found later. Service centres are open from Saturday to
Thursday, from 10:00 to 18:00; the document on service hours has the details.

If the number is registered to someone else in your family, that person has to come to the service
centre for the replacement. If you are abroad when the SIM is lost, block it from abroad by calling
`01700000009`, and replace it at a service centre when you are back in Bangladesh.

## If you find the SIM again

If you find your SIM after blocking it and you have not replaced it yet, you can have it unblocked.
Unblocking is done only at a Nodi Telecom service centre, by the registered owner of the number, in
person, with their own national ID card. It cannot be done by phone, in the app or through the
helpline, and the account PIN cannot be used to unblock a SIM.

This rule is strict on purpose. Anyone who calls to unblock a SIM might be the person who took it,
so an unblock is only done when the staff can see the registered owner and check the ID card. After
the unblock, the SIM works again at once, with your main balance and any packs that have not yet
reached their end date.

If you have already replaced the SIM, the old SIM cannot be unblocked; it stays blocked for good.
Cut it up and throw it away. If you are not sure whether your SIM is blocked or already replaced,
ask customer care on the helpline from another phone, or ask at a service centre.
