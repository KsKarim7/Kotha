# Replacing a SIM

## Who can replace a SIM

A SIM can be replaced only by the registered owner of the number, in person. The registered owner is
the person whose national ID card was used when the number was registered. You need a replacement
when your SIM is lost, stolen, damaged or no longer read by your phone, or when you need a SIM of a
different size for a new phone.

Nobody else can replace a SIM for the owner. Nodi Telecom does not accept letters of authorisation,
copies of an ID card or a phone call from the owner instead of the owner coming in person. This rule
protects your number: whoever holds your SIM can receive your calls and SMS, so a replacement is
given only to the person the number belongs to.

If the number is registered to someone else in your family, that person has to come for the
replacement. If you are not sure in whose name your number is registered, ask customer care on the
helpline; for your safety, they will not give the full name over the phone, but they can tell you
whether the number is registered to you once you have passed the PIN check described in the
document on account PIN and verification.

If your SIM was lost or stolen, block it first by calling the helpline, as the document on a lost or
stolen SIM explains, and then come in for the replacement.

## Where to go and what to bring

SIM replacements are done only at Nodi Telecom service centres. Retailers that sell recharges cannot
replace a SIM, and a replacement cannot be ordered in the app or by phone. Service centres are open
from Saturday to Thursday, from 10:00 to 18:00, and are closed on Fridays and public holidays; the
document on service hours has the details.

Bring your own national ID card, the original and not a copy. The staff at the service centre check
that the card matches the registration of the number and that you are the registered owner. If the
card does not match, the replacement cannot be done.

If you still have the old SIM, bring it as well. It is not required, but it helps the staff check
the number. The replacement itself usually takes a few minutes at the counter. The new SIM is
usually active within an hour after it is handed to you; you receive an SMS on the new SIM when it is
active. Until then, keep the new SIM in your phone and do not try to recharge or buy packs.

## What you keep and what happens to the old SIM

A replacement SIM keeps everything that belongs to your number:

- the same phone number;
- your main balance;
- your active packs, with their data or minutes left and their original end dates;
- your main account validity, which is not changed by the replacement;
- your account PIN and your auto-renew settings.

Only the physical SIM changes. A replacement is not a recharge, so it does not restore the 90 days of
main account validity; the document on validity explains how that works.

The old SIM stops working as soon as the new SIM is active. From that moment the old SIM cannot make
or receive calls, send or receive SMS, use data or be recharged, even if it is found again later. If
you find your old SIM after the replacement, cut it up and throw it away. If you replaced the SIM
because it was lost or stolen, the block on the old SIM stays in place and you do not need to do
anything more.

## The replacement fee

A replacement fee may apply. The fee can change, so this page does not list it. Customer care can
tell you the current fee on the helpline, and the Nodi Telecom app shows it under SIM services.

The fee is paid at the service centre when the replacement is done. It is not taken from your main
balance, so your main balance stays exactly as it was. If you are asked to pay a replacement fee by
anyone outside a service centre, for example by phone or SMS, do not pay: Nodi Telecom only takes the
fee at the counter of a service centre.

If you think you were charged a replacement fee wrongly, you can make a complaint as described in
the document on complaints. Refunds for wrong charges are explained in the document on refunds.
Keep the receipt that the service centre gives you, because customer care will ask for it.

A SIM that stops working because of a fault in the SIM itself, within a short time after you got it,
may be replaced without a fee. Ask at the service centre; the staff will check the SIM and tell you
whether the fee applies.
