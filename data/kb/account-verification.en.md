# Account PIN and verification

## Your account PIN

Your account PIN is a 4-digit code that proves you are the owner of your Nodi Telecom number when you
call us. It is separate from your mobile wallet PIN and from the PIN that locks your SIM in your
phone's settings; do not use the same code for all of them.

You set your account PIN in the Nodi Telecom app, under account security, and you can change it there
at any time. Choose digits that other people cannot guess easily: avoid your birthday, your phone
number, repeated digits and simple sequences. When you set or change the PIN, you receive an SMS
confirming the change. If you receive that SMS without having changed your PIN, call the helpline at
once.

Until you set an account PIN, the voice assistant and customer care can give you only general
information on a call, such as how packs work or what a rule says. They cannot tell you anything
about your own account or change anything on it.

You do not need the PIN to check your own balance or to buy packs from your own phone. Checking
your balance with `*747*1#`, `*747*2#` or `*747*3#`, sending `BAL`, `DATA` or `MIN` to `7470`, and
using the app all work without the PIN, because they only work from your own number.

## When you are asked for it

When you call the helpline yourself, the voice assistant or customer care asks for two things before
telling you anything about your account or changing anything on it: your phone number and your
account PIN. Only after both are checked do they tell you, for example, your balance, your active
packs or the history of your recharges, or do things for you such as turning auto-renew on or off.

You are asked for the PIN only on a call that you make to the helpline: `747` from a Nodi Telecom
number, or `01700000009` from any other phone. Nodi Telecom never calls you to ask for your PIN. If
someone calls you, says they are from Nodi Telecom and asks for your PIN, end the call and call the
helpline yourself.

Say or type the PIN only when the voice assistant or customer care asks for it. You can enter it with
the keypad instead of saying it aloud, which is safer in a public place. Customer care never needs to
hear your PIN more than once on the same call.

Some requests always need a visit to a service centre with your national ID card, whatever PIN you
give: replacing a SIM and unblocking a blocked SIM. The documents on replacing a SIM and on a lost or
stolen SIM explain why. Blocking a lost or stolen SIM is the one change that customer care makes
without your PIN, because it only stops the SIM from being used.

## What Nodi Telecom never asks for

Nodi Telecom never asks for your wallet PIN, any password or a one-time code, and never asks for your
account PIN by SMS, email or a link. Your account PIN is asked for only when you call the helpline
yourself. If anyone else asks for it, end the conversation and call the helpline yourself.

In particular, Nodi Telecom never:

- sends an SMS or email asking you to reply with your PIN;
- sends a link to a page where you are asked to enter your PIN;
- asks for a one-time code that you received by SMS;
- asks for your mobile wallet PIN or your bank card details.

Never share your account PIN with anyone, including family members, retailers and people who say
they work for Nodi Telecom. Do not write it on your phone or on the SIM card holder. If you think
someone else knows your PIN, change it in the app at once. If you cannot use the app, go to a service
centre with your national ID card.

## Wrong PIN attempts and a locked PIN

If you enter a wrong PIN three times in a row on calls to the helpline, verification by phone is
locked for 24 hours. During that time the voice assistant and customer care can still give general
information, but they cannot tell you anything about your account or change anything on it. You can
still check your balance and buy packs from your own phone, because that does not need the PIN.

You can reset your PIN at any time, without waiting for the lock to end:

- in the Nodi Telecom app, under account security;
- at a Nodi Telecom service centre, as the registered owner, with your own national ID card.

When the PIN is reset, the lock ends at once, and you receive an SMS confirming the reset. If your
PIN was locked although you did not make the wrong attempts yourself, someone may be trying to get
into your account: reset the PIN, and tell customer care. Remember that a blocked SIM is a different
matter: it can be unblocked only at a service centre, never with the PIN.
