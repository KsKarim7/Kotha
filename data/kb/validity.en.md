# Account and pack validity

## Main account validity

Your main account validity is the period during which your number stays fully active. While it is
fully active you can make and receive calls, send and receive SMS, use the internet, and buy packs.

Each recharge, of any amount, makes your main account fully active for 90 days from the moment of
that recharge. If you recharge again before the 90 days are over, the 90 days start again from the
new recharge; they are not added on top of each other. For example, if you recharge on the first day
of a month and again on the tenth day, your account stays fully active for 90 days from the tenth
day.

You can see the date until which your account is fully active when you check your main balance:
dial `*747*1#`, look at the app's home screen, or send `BAL` to `7470`. Nodi Telecom also sends you an
SMS seven days before the 90 days end, and another one on the last day, as a reminder to recharge.

Main account validity is separate from the validity of your packs. Buying a pack does not extend
your main account validity, because a pack is paid from your existing main balance and is not a
recharge. Only a recharge restores the 90 days.

## Pack validity

Every pack has its own validity, which is fixed for each pack: 3, 7 or 30 days, as listed in the
documents on internet, voice and combo packages. A pack's validity starts at the moment the pack is
activated and is counted in full days, so a pack bought at 18:30 ends at 18:30 on its last day.

The end date and time of each active pack are shown when you check your data balance or your minute
balance, and they are also in the SMS you receive when you buy the pack. A pack can be used until
its end date, or until its data or minutes run out, whichever comes first.

Pack validity and main account validity run independently, but a pack can only be used while your
main account is fully active. If your main account validity ends while a pack is still active, the
pack keeps its end date but cannot be used until you recharge. After a recharge the pack works again
for whatever time it has left; the days that passed while the account was not fully active are not
added back.

Each pack also runs independently of your other packs. Having several packs does not change the
validity of any of them, and the pack that ends soonest is used first.

## What happens when each expires

**When the main account validity ends**, your number moves into an incoming-only period of 30 days.
During that period you can still receive calls and SMS, but you cannot make calls, send SMS, use the
internet or buy packs. Your main balance is kept, and so are your active packs, but neither can be
used. Calls to the helpline `747` still work.

If you do not recharge during the 30-day incoming-only period, your number can be closed. A closed
number cannot receive calls, and any main balance left on it is lost. Nodi Telecom sends an SMS at
the start of the incoming-only period and another one seven days before it ends.

**When a pack's validity ends**, the data or minutes left in it expire. Unused data and minutes do
not carry over to another pack and cannot be refunded. If auto-renew is on, the same pack is bought
again at that moment, as long as your main balance is enough; otherwise the pack simply ends and you
receive an SMS. The end of a pack does not affect your main balance, your main account validity or
your other packs.

## Extending validity

**Main account validity.** Any recharge restores the full 90 days, counted from the moment of the
recharge. A recharge during the incoming-only period restores full service at once, together with
your main balance and any active packs. Once a number has been closed, a recharge no longer restores
it; contact customer care, although getting a closed number back may not be possible.

**Pack validity.** A pack's validity cannot be extended on its own. There are two ways to keep a
pack going:

- buy the same pack again before it ends: the data or minutes are added up, and the validity becomes
  the later of the two end dates;
- turn auto-renew on, so that the same pack is bought again automatically when its validity ends.

Buying a different pack does not extend an existing pack; the two packs run side by side with their
own end dates. Recharging does not extend a pack either, although it does keep your main account
fully active, which a pack needs in order to work. The document on buying, activating and stopping
packs explains how to buy a pack again and how to turn auto-renew on and off.
