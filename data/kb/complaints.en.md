# Complaints

## How to make a complaint

If something has gone wrong with your Nodi Telecom service, such as a charge you do not recognise, a
pack that was not activated, a recharge that did not arrive or a problem with the network, you can
make a complaint in three ways:

- **The helpline.** Call `747` from a Nodi Telecom number, or `01700000009` from any other phone. The
  helpline is open 24 hours, every day. For a complaint about your own account, customer care first
  checks your phone number and your account PIN, as the document on account PIN and verification
  describes.
- **The app.** Open the Help section of the Nodi Telecom app and choose "Make a complaint". You can
  describe the problem in writing and attach a screenshot, for example of an error message.
- **A service centre.** Visit a Nodi Telecom service centre with your national ID card. Service
  centres are open from Saturday to Thursday, from 10:00 to 18:00; the document on service hours has
  the details.

All three channels lead to the same complaint system, so it does not matter which one you use. Make
one complaint for each problem. If you make the same complaint twice, the second one is joined to the
first and keeps the first ticket number. Some problems have their own quick steps that may solve
them without a complaint; for recharges, try the steps in the document on recharge problems first.

## What to have ready

A complaint is resolved faster when customer care has all the details at once. Before you call, write
in the app or visit a service centre, have these ready:

- your Nodi Telecom number;
- what went wrong, in a few words;
- the date and time when it happened, as exactly as you can;
- for a charge: the pack code or the kind of charge, and what the history in the app shows;
- for a recharge: the method you used and the transaction ID, receipt or voucher number;
- for a network problem: the place where it happened, and whether calls, SMS or data were affected;
- the ticket number, if you have already made a complaint about the same problem.

Keep any SMS you received about the problem, such as a purchase confirmation or an error message, and
take screenshots in the app if you can.

Never give anyone your wallet PIN, any password or a one-time code, also not while making a
complaint. Customer care asks for your account PIN only when you call the helpline yourself, and only
to check that you are the owner of the number.

## Your ticket number

Every complaint gets a ticket number. You receive it by SMS within a few minutes of making the
complaint, whichever channel you used. A ticket number has the form `TKT-` followed by 6 digits, for
example TKT-000001.

Keep the ticket number until the complaint is closed. Customer care asks for it whenever you follow
up, and it lets anyone at Nodi Telecom find your complaint at once without you having to explain the
problem again. The ticket number stays the same for the whole complaint, also if it is escalated.

If you do not receive the SMS with the ticket number within an hour, call the helpline: customer care
can find the complaint by your number and tell you the ticket number. Nodi Telecom sends ticket
numbers only by SMS from Nodi Telecom and never asks you to pay anything to open or follow up a
complaint. If a message with a ticket number asks you for a PIN, a password, a one-time code or a
payment, it is not from Nodi Telecom: do not answer it, and call the helpline.

## When you will get a reply

Nodi Telecom replies to every complaint within 3 working days. Working days are Saturday to Thursday,
except public holidays, the same days on which the service centres are open. The reply comes by SMS
or by a call from customer care, and it tells you what was found and what happens next.

Many complaints are answered sooner, often on the same day. If the complaint needs more time, for
example because a payment provider or a partner network has to be asked, the reply within 3 working
days tells you so and says when you can expect the final answer.

If the investigation finds that you were charged wrongly, the charge is refunded to your main balance
as the document on refunds describes. When the complaint is resolved, you receive an SMS saying that
the ticket is closed. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can still escalate the complaint,
as described below, also after the ticket has been closed.

## Following up and escalating

To follow up a complaint, call the helpline or open the complaint in the Help section of the app,
and give your ticket number. Customer care tells you where the complaint stands. There is no need to
make a new complaint about the same problem; that only creates a second ticket that is joined to the
first.

You can escalate a complaint when:

- you have had no reply within 3 working days;
- you are not satisfied with the answer;
- the problem was said to be solved, but it has come back.

To escalate, call the helpline or visit a service centre, give your ticket number, and ask for the
complaint to be escalated. It then goes to Nodi Telecom's senior complaints team, which reviews the
whole case and replies within 5 working days. The ticket number stays the same, and you receive an
SMS when the complaint has been escalated. The senior team's answer is Nodi Telecom's final answer to
the complaint.
