# Checking your balance

## Main balance

Your main balance is the money on your Nodi Telecom account. It is used to buy packs, to pay for
calls when you have no pack minutes left or when a call is not covered by a pack, and to pay for SMS.
Every recharge adds to it.

You can check your main balance in three ways, all of them free:

- dial `*747*1#`;
- open the Nodi Telecom app, where the main balance is shown at the top of the home screen;
- send `BAL` by SMS to `7470`.

The answer shows two things: the amount on your main balance, and the date until which your main
account stays fully active. That date is your main account validity; the document on validity
explains how it works and how a recharge extends it. The USSD answer appears on screen within a few
seconds, and the SMS answer usually arrives within a minute.

Packs are not part of the main balance. A pack you have bought has its own data or minutes, which
you check separately, as described below. If your main balance looks lower than you expected, open
the app: its history lists every recharge, every pack purchase and every charged call, with the date
and time of each.

## Data balance

Your data balance shows how much internet data you have left in your active packs. You can check it
in three ways, all of them free:

- dial `*747*2#`;
- open the Nodi Telecom app and choose "My packs";
- send `DATA` by SMS to `7470`.

The answer lists every active internet pack and every combo pack with data, one line each. Each line
shows the pack code, the data left, and the date and time when the pack ends. The night pack,
NT-NIGHT-5GB, is shown on its own line with its hours, 00:00 to 08:00, so you can tell its data apart
from data you can use during the day.

If you have several packs, the pack that ends soonest is used first, so its line is the one that goes
down first. If no internet pack is active, the answer says so and tells you how to buy one. The data
balance does not show your main balance, because the main balance is not used for internet: when all
pack data is used up, your internet stops until you buy a new pack. Check your data balance before a
long download, and a day or two before a pack ends, so that unused data does not expire unnoticed.

## Minute balance

Your minute balance shows how many call minutes you have left in your active packs. You can check it
in three ways, all of them free:

- dial `*747*3#`;
- open the Nodi Telecom app and choose "My packs";
- send `MIN` by SMS to `7470`.

The answer lists every active voice pack and every combo pack with minutes, one line each. Each line
shows the pack code, the minutes left, and the date and time when the pack ends. Minutes are counted
per second, so the minutes left can include part of a minute, shown in minutes and seconds.

If you have several packs with minutes, the pack that ends soonest is used first. The minutes cover
local calls inside Bangladesh to every operator, on-net and off-net; calls outside that, such as
international calls, are charged from your main balance even while you have minutes left. When all
pack minutes are finished, calls are charged from your main balance at the standard call rate. To see
how much a call has used, check your minute balance before and after it.

## How to reach us

These are all the codes and numbers Nodi Telecom uses. Every other document uses the same ones.

| What you want | How |
| --- | --- |
| The main USSD menu | Dial `*747#` |
| Main balance | Dial `*747*1#`, or send `BAL` to `7470` |
| Data balance | Dial `*747*2#`, or send `DATA` to `7470` |
| Minute balance | Dial `*747*3#`, or send `MIN` to `7470` |
| Buy, stop or auto-renew a pack | Dial `*747*4#` |
| Recharge with a voucher | Dial `*747*5*<voucher number>#` |
| Buy a pack by SMS | Send the pack code, for example `NT-30GB`, to `7470` |
| Stop a pack by SMS | Send `STOP` and the pack code to `7470` |
| Auto-renew by SMS | Send `AUTO ON` or `AUTO OFF` and the pack code to `7470` |
| Helpline from a Nodi Telecom number | Call `747`, free of charge |
| Helpline from any other phone | Call `01700000009` |

The Nodi Telecom app has the same functions, and its "Help" section lets you write to customer care.
SMS to `7470` and calls to `747` are free. Nodi Telecom staff will never ask for your PIN, your wallet
PIN or any password; if someone does, end the conversation and call the helpline yourself.
