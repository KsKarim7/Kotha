# Ways to recharge

## Recharging in the app

The quickest way to recharge is the Nodi Telecom app. Open the app and choose "Recharge". Choose
the number to recharge, which can be your own number or any other Nodi Telecom number, then enter
the amount and choose how to pay: a bank card or a mobile wallet linked to the app. Check the number
and the amount, then confirm.

The main balance is usually updated within a few seconds. You receive an SMS that confirms the
recharge and shows your new main balance, and the app shows the same on its home screen. The app
keeps a history of your recharges, with the date, the time and a transaction ID for each one. Keep
the transaction ID if anything goes wrong, because customer care will ask for it.

The app shows the smallest and the largest amount you can recharge at a time. These limits can
change, so check them in the app rather than relying on an old screenshot. Check the number
carefully before you confirm a recharge for someone else: a recharge to the wrong number cannot
always be reversed. The document on recharge problems explains what to do if that happens. Each
recharge also restores the full validity of your main account, as described in the document on
validity.

## Recharging with a voucher by USSD

Recharge vouchers are sold by retailers. Each voucher has a hidden voucher number under a scratch
panel. To recharge with a voucher:

1. Scratch the panel gently and read the voucher number.
2. Dial `*747*5*<voucher number>#`, with the voucher number in place of `<voucher number>`, and
   press call.
3. Wait for the confirmation on screen and the SMS that shows your new main balance.

A voucher can be used only once, and only on a Nodi Telecom number. If you type the voucher number
wrongly, you receive an error message; check the digits and try again. After five wrong attempts
in a row, recharging with a voucher is locked on your number for 24 hours, to protect against
guessing. Recharging in the app or at a retailer still works during that time.

Keep the voucher until the recharge is confirmed. If the screen says the voucher has already been
used but you have not used it, do not throw it away: contact customer care with the voucher number
and the place where you bought it.

## Recharging at a retailer

Shops that show the Nodi Telecom sign can recharge your number directly, without a voucher. Tell the
retailer your number and the amount you want to recharge. The retailer enters them on their own
phone or terminal, and the recharge reaches your main balance within a few seconds.

Before the retailer confirms, check that the number on their screen is your number. Many problems
with retailer recharges come from one wrong digit. When the recharge arrives you receive an SMS with
your new main balance; wait for it before you leave the shop if you can. Note the time of the
recharge, and ask for a receipt if the shop gives one.

If the SMS does not arrive and your main balance has not changed after 15 minutes, follow the steps
in the document on recharge problems. Retailers can recharge any Nodi Telecom number, so you can
also recharge a family member's number at a shop. Retailers are not allowed to ask for your PIN or
any password, and you never need to give them anything other than the number to recharge.

## Recharging from a mobile wallet

You can recharge your number from a mobile wallet. Open the wallet's app or menu and choose its
mobile recharge option. Choose Nodi Telecom as the operator, enter the number to recharge and the
amount, check them, and confirm with your wallet PIN. The recharge usually reaches your main balance
within a few seconds, and you receive an SMS from Nodi Telecom with your new main balance.

The wallet also shows a transaction ID for the recharge. Keep it until the recharge has arrived,
because customer care and the wallet provider will both ask for it if something goes wrong.

Your wallet PIN belongs to you alone. Never share it with anyone, including anyone who says they
work for Nodi Telecom: Nodi Telecom never asks for your wallet PIN, by phone, SMS or any other way.
If the payment has left your wallet but the recharge has not arrived after 15 minutes, see the
document on recharge problems. If you linked the wallet to the Nodi Telecom app, you can also choose
it as the payment method when you recharge in the app.
