# Recharge problems

## A failed recharge

A recharge has failed when you see an error message instead of a confirmation, or when no
confirmation SMS arrives and your main balance has not changed. The most common reasons are a
mistyped number, a busy network, a declined card or wallet payment, and a voucher that has already
been used.

First check your main balance with `*747*1#`, in the app, or by sending `BAL` to `7470`. If the
balance has gone up, the recharge has worked and only the SMS is late. If the balance has not
changed, the recharge has failed and nothing has been added to your account.

If you paid by card or mobile wallet and the payment was taken but the recharge failed, the payment
is normally returned to your card or wallet by the payment provider. How long that takes depends on
the provider, not on Nodi Telecom. Keep the transaction ID until the payment has come back.

Try the recharge once more after a few minutes, checking the number and the amount carefully. If it
fails again, do not keep trying: contact customer care with the details listed at the end of this
document. Repeated attempts with a voucher can lock voucher recharges on your number for 24 hours.

## Recharging the wrong number

If you recharged the wrong number by mistake, contact customer care as soon as possible, and within
24 hours of the recharge. Have these details ready: your own number, the number that was recharged,
the time of the recharge, the method you used, and the transaction ID, receipt or voucher number.

Customer care checks the recharge with you. If the wrong number belongs to Nodi Telecom and the
balance on it has not been used, customer care may be able to move the recharge to the right
number. A reversal is not promised: once the other person has used the balance, it cannot be taken
back, and after 24 hours a reversal is usually not possible at all.

If you recharged a number of another operator through a retailer or a mobile wallet, Nodi Telecom
cannot reverse it, because the recharge never reached Nodi Telecom. Contact the retailer or the
wallet provider instead. Do not contact the owner of the wrong number yourself to ask for anything;
let customer care handle it. To avoid this problem, always read the number back before you confirm
a recharge, especially when you recharge someone else's number.

## A recharge that hasn't arrived after 15 minutes

Most recharges reach your main balance within a few seconds, but sometimes the network is slow. If
15 minutes have passed and you have not received the confirmation SMS, follow these steps:

1. Check your main balance with `*747*1#`, in the app, or by sending `BAL` to `7470`. The recharge
   may have arrived even if the SMS has not.
2. If the balance has not changed, restart your phone and check again, because the phone may not
   have received the update.
3. Keep the proof of the recharge: the voucher, the retailer's receipt, or the transaction ID from
   the app or the wallet.
4. Contact customer care on the helpline with that proof and the time of the recharge.

Do not use the same voucher again and do not ask the retailer to repeat the recharge until customer
care has checked the first one; otherwise you may pay twice. Customer care can see whether the
recharge reached Nodi Telecom and will tell you what happens next.

## When to contact customer care

Contact customer care when:

- a recharge has not arrived after 15 minutes and your main balance has not changed;
- you recharged the wrong number;
- a payment was taken from your card or wallet but the recharge did not arrive;
- a voucher says it has already been used, but you have not used it;
- recharges fail again and again, or voucher recharges are locked on your number.

Call the helpline on `747` from a Nodi Telecom number, which is free, or on `01700000009` from any
other phone. You can also open "Help" in the Nodi Telecom app. Have your number ready, together with
the time of the recharge, the method you used, and the transaction ID, receipt or voucher number.

Nodi Telecom never asks for your wallet PIN, any password or a one-time code, and never asks for your
account PIN by SMS, email or a link. Your account PIN is asked for only when you call the helpline
yourself; see the document on account PIN and verification. If anyone else asks for it, end the
conversation and call the helpline yourself. All codes and numbers are listed under "How to reach
us" in the document on checking your balance.
