# Buying, activating and stopping packs

## Buying and activating a pack

Every internet, voice and combo pack can be bought in three ways. Whichever way you choose, the
pack is activated at once, and its validity starts from that moment.

- **In the app.** Open the Nodi Telecom app, go to the packs page, choose a pack, check the details
  and confirm. The app shows the current price before you confirm.
- **By USSD.** Dial `*747*4#`, choose the type of pack, choose the pack and confirm. The menu shows
  the current price before you confirm.
- **By SMS.** Send the pack code, for example `NT-15GB` or `NC-10GB`, to `7470`. The pack is
  activated as soon as the message is received.

The price is taken from your main balance. If your main balance is too low, the purchase fails,
nothing is taken, and you receive an SMS that says so; recharge and try again. After a successful
purchase you receive an SMS with the pack's code, its data or minutes, and its end date and time.
Keep this SMS until the pack ends.

If you do not receive the SMS within a few minutes, check your data balance or minute balance to see
whether the pack is active before you try to buy it again. Buying the same pack twice is allowed
and is described below, but it is better to check first than to buy a second pack by accident.

## Stopping a pack

You can stop an active pack at any time, for example if you bought the wrong pack. Stopping a pack
ends it at once: any data or minutes left in it expire immediately and are not refunded, and
auto-renew for that pack is turned off.

- **In the app:** open "My packs", choose the pack and select "Stop pack".
- **By USSD:** dial `*747*4#`, choose "My packs", then the pack, then "Stop".
- **By SMS:** send `STOP` followed by the pack code, for example `STOP NT-30GB`, to `7470`.

You receive an SMS confirming that the pack has stopped. If you only want the pack not to renew,
you do not need to stop it: turn auto-renew off instead, and the pack keeps working until its end
date. Stopping a pack does not affect your other packs or your main balance.

A stopped pack cannot be restarted. If you stopped a pack by mistake, buy it again; the new pack
starts with its full data or minutes and a new end date. If you believe a pack was stopped without
your request, contact customer care on the helpline and give the pack code and the time you noticed
it.

## Auto-renew

Auto-renew buys the same pack again automatically when its validity ends, so that you are not left
without data or minutes at the end of the period. Auto-renew is off by default for every pack. It
works only at the end of the validity: if the data or minutes run out earlier, the pack does not
renew until its end date.

To turn auto-renew on:

- in the app, open "My packs", choose the pack and switch on "Auto-renew";
- by USSD, dial `*747*4#`, choose "My packs", the pack, then "Auto-renew on";
- by SMS, send `AUTO ON` followed by the pack code, for example `AUTO ON NT-30GB`, to `7470`.

To turn it off, follow the same steps and choose "Auto-renew off", or send `AUTO OFF` followed by
the pack code to `7470`. Turning auto-renew off does not stop the pack; it keeps working until its
end date.

When a pack renews, the current price is taken from your main balance and you receive an SMS with
the new end date. If your main balance is too low at that moment, the renewal fails, the pack ends
normally, auto-renew is turned off for that pack, and you receive an SMS that says so.

## Buying the same pack twice

You can buy a pack again while the same pack is still active. In that case the two purchases are
combined into one pack:

- the data or minutes left from the first purchase are kept, and the new data or minutes are added
  to them;
- the validity becomes the later of the two end dates, which is the end date of the new purchase.

For example, if you have 4 GB left in NT-30GB with 5 days to go and you buy NT-30GB again, you will
have 34 GB, valid for 30 days from the second purchase. For a combo, the data and the minutes are
each added up in the same way, and both parts share the later end date.

Buying a different pack does not combine anything: the two packs run side by side, each with its own
data or minutes and its own end date, and the pack that ends soonest is used first. Buying the same
pack twice is useful when the data runs out before the end date and you want to stay on the same
pack for the rest of the period. The second purchase costs the current price of the pack, taken
from your main balance.
