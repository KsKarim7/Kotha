# International roaming

## Turning international roaming on

International roaming lets you use your Nodi Telecom number abroad, on a partner network in the
country you visit. It is off by default on every number, so that nobody is charged for roaming by
accident. Turn it on before you travel, while you are still in Bangladesh:

- in the Nodi Telecom app, open the roaming page and switch on "International roaming";
- or call the helpline, `747` from your Nodi Telecom number, and ask customer care to turn it on.
  Customer care checks your phone number and your account PIN first, as the document on account PIN
  and verification describes.

Roaming is usually active within a few minutes, and you receive an SMS when it is on. Your main
account must be fully active for roaming to work, so check your main account validity before you
travel; the document on validity explains how it works.

When you arrive abroad, your phone connects to a partner network automatically. If it does not,
restart the phone, or choose a network by hand in your phone's network settings. When you are back in
Bangladesh, you can turn roaming off again in the app or by calling the helpline. If you leave it on,
nothing is charged while you are at home; roaming is charged only when you use a network abroad.

## Packs and charges abroad

Local packs do not work abroad. The data and minutes in your internet, voice and combo packs can be
used only inside Bangladesh. While you are abroad they are not used, but they keep their end dates,
so a pack can expire while you are away. Check the end dates of your packs before you travel.

Abroad, everything you use is charged from your main balance at roaming rates:

- outgoing calls, both to Bangladesh and to local numbers in the country you visit;
- incoming calls, which are also charged while roaming;
- SMS that you send;
- mobile data.

Roaming rates depend on the country and the partner network, and they can change, so this page does
not list them. The current rates for each country are shown on the roaming page of the Nodi Telecom
app, and customer care can tell you the rates on the helpline. Make sure your main balance is enough
before you travel; you can also recharge from abroad in the app.

Roaming charges appear in the history of your main balance in the app, usually within a day. Calls
made while roaming are never taken from the minutes in a voice pack, as the document on voice
packages says.

## Avoiding unexpected charges

Mobile data is the most common cause of high roaming charges, because phones use data in the
background for app updates, backups and messages. To avoid unexpected charges, turn data roaming off
when you do not need it. There are two switches, and it is safest to use both:

- the data roaming switch in your phone's settings, which stops the phone from using data abroad;
- the data roaming switch on the roaming page of the Nodi Telecom app, which turns off roaming data on
  your number while keeping roaming calls and SMS on.

With data roaming off, you can still make and receive calls and send SMS at roaming rates, and you
can use Wi-Fi for the internet without any roaming charge. You can turn data roaming on again at any
time, for example only when you need a map.

Other ways to keep costs down: let calls you do not need go to voicemail, keep calls short, and check
your main balance regularly with `*747*1#`. If you think you were charged wrongly for roaming, make a
complaint as described in the document on complaints.

## Calling us from abroad

The helpline number `747` works only inside Bangladesh. From abroad, call the helpline on
`01700000009`, dialled with the Bangladesh country code if your phone needs it. The call is charged
as a roaming call from your main balance. The helpline is open 24 hours, every day.

You can also use the Help section of the Nodi Telecom app abroad, over Wi-Fi, to write to customer
care without any roaming charge.

If your SIM or phone is lost or stolen abroad, block the SIM at once by calling `01700000009` from
any phone, as the document on a lost or stolen SIM explains. A replacement SIM can be given only at a
service centre in Bangladesh, to the registered owner with their own national ID card, so the
replacement has to wait until you are back.

For any other question about roaming, such as whether roaming works in a particular country, ask
customer care on the helpline or check the roaming page in the app before you travel.
