# Combo packages

## Combo packs

A combo pack gives internet data and call minutes in one purchase. Nodi Telecom offers two combo
packs, each valid for 30 days from activation.

| Code | Data | Minutes | Validity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NC-10GB | 10 GB | 200 | 30 days | Data and minutes for a regular month |
| NC-25GB | 25 GB | 500 | 30 days | Our largest combo, for heavy use |

The data in a combo works exactly like the data in an internet pack: it can be used on 4G and 3G
for browsing, apps, video and calls over the internet. The minutes work exactly like the minutes in
a voice pack: they cover local calls inside Bangladesh to every operator, on-net and off-net, and
they are counted per second. A combo is valid only on the number it was bought for, and neither its
data nor its minutes can be moved to another number.

A combo is a good choice when you need both data and minutes every month and would rather make one
purchase than two. If you mostly use data, or mostly make calls, a separate internet or voice pack
may suit you better. The documents on internet packages and voice packages list those packs.

## How the data and minutes are used

The data and the minutes in a combo are two separate parts, used independently. Using up the data
does not reduce the minutes, and using up the minutes does not reduce the data. You receive an SMS
when 80 percent of either part has been used, and another SMS when that part is finished.

When the data in a combo is finished, your internet stops, just as with an internet pack, and your
main balance is not charged for internet use. When the minutes are finished, calls are charged from
your main balance at the standard call rate, just as with a voice pack. The other part keeps working
until it is used up or the combo ends.

If you have a combo and an internet or voice pack active at the same time, the usual rule applies
to each part: for data, the active pack with data that ends soonest is used first, and for minutes,
the active pack with minutes that ends soonest is used first. Your data balance and your minute
balance show the combo's data and minutes on separate lines, each with the combo's end date, so you
can see what is left in each part at any time.

## When a combo expires

A combo ends 30 days after it is activated, at the same time of day. When it ends, its data and its
minutes expire together, even if one of the two parts has not been used. Unused data and minutes do
not carry over to the next pack and cannot be refunded.

If you buy the same combo again before it ends, the data and the minutes left in it are kept, and the
new amounts are added to them. Both parts then share the later of the two end dates. Buying a
different combo, or a separate internet or voice pack, does not change the combo's end date.

If auto-renew is on, the combo renews when its validity ends, as long as your main balance is
enough at that moment. Auto-renew does not renew a combo early when one part runs out; in that case
you can buy a separate internet or voice pack to cover the rest of the month. Auto-renew is off by
default; see the document on buying, activating and stopping packs for how to turn it on and off.

## Prices and how to buy

Prices of combo packs can change, so this page does not list them. The current price of each combo
is shown in the Nodi Telecom app, and customer care can tell you the current price on the helpline.
The price is taken from your main balance when the combo is activated.

You can buy a combo in three ways:

- In the Nodi Telecom app, open the packs page, choose the combo and confirm.
- Dial `*747*4#`, choose combo packs, then choose the combo and confirm.
- Send the combo code, for example `NC-10GB`, by SMS to `7470`.

After a successful purchase you receive a confirmation SMS with the combo's data, its minutes and
its end date. If the purchase fails, nothing is taken from your main balance, and the SMS explains
why. You can check what is left with `*747*2#` for data and `*747*3#` for minutes, in the app, or by
sending `DATA` or `MIN` to `7470`.
